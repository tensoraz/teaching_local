Missing values

Why is it missing? MCAR · MAR · MNAR

MCAR
Missing Completely At Random
Pure chance: a form page didn’t load.

Safe to drop a few rows.
MAR
Missing At Random (given other data)
Age missing 6.0% for prepaid, 1.4% for postpaid.

Fill using the related column.
MNAR
Missing Not At Random
Unhappy customers skip the survey: churn 27.8% when blank vs 22.1%.

Keep a “was missing” flag.

You can test MCAR vs MAR with the data. MNAR can only be suspected: the missing value itself is what you cannot see.

Outliers · maths

Detecting outliers: IQR and z-score

IQR = Q3 − Q1 = 43.65 − 21.90 = 21.75
fences = Q1 − 1.5·IQR, Q3 + 1.5·IQR
= −10.73 and 76.28 → 28 flagged
z = (x − x̄) / s, flag if |z| > 3
x̄ = 37.76, s = 59.18
z(999.9) = 962.1 / 59.18 = 16.3
|z| > 3 → 6 flagged
IQR flags 28: 6 glitches + 22 real heavy users. z misses the heavy users: the glitches inflated s (masking). Domain rule: no plan allows > 100 GB.

Outlier treatment: first ask why

CauseSwiftTel exampleTreatment
Typo you can explaincharge 59,900 (an extra “00”)Fix: ÷ 100 → 599

Genuine but extremeheavy user, 87.5 GBKeep; maybe cap (winsorize) or log-transform
Different populationa corporate SIM with 500 linesSeparate segment or model
The thing you wantfraud, network attackKeep: it is the signal

Capping at the 1st/99th percentile = winsorizing. Always record what you changed and why.

Transformation · maths

Scaling: put features on the same footing

Min-max: x′ = (x − min) / (max − min)
(599 − 199) / (999 − 199) = 0.50
z-score: z = (x − x̄) / s
(599 − 474.55) / 211.44 = 0.589
Min-max → range 0 to 1. Hurt by outliers.
z-score (standardization) → mean 0, sd 1. Default for most models.
Needed for KNN, K-means, logistic, neural nets. Not needed for decision trees.
